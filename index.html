I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Keio University, advised by Prof. Yasushi Narushima. My research interests lie broadly in continuous optimization, dynamical systems, and machine learning, with a focus on applying these techniques to establish theoretical foundations for AI. I am particularly interested in developing methods to enhance the safety and reliability of neural networks.

Previously, I completed my B.S. and M.S. degrees in mathematics, during which I studied numerical schemes for solving a PDE. This work naturally led me to develop an interest in optimization algorithms and their theoretical foundations. Taking the continuous-time limit reveals connections between numerical schemes and optimization algorithms through ODEs / SDEs. This connection has motivated me to approach my research from a dynamical systems perspective.

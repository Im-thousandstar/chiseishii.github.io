Chise Ishii

Ph.D. Student @ Keio University

In my spare time, I enjoy playing the violin. I began studying the violin at the age of five, and classical music has been an important part of my life ever since. In 2013, I appeared as a soloist at the Altenburger Musikfestival. As an undergraduate, I served as concertmaster for the Waseda Symphony Orchestra's 2018 European tour, performing in 12 cities, including the Berlin Philharmonie, the Elbphilharmonie in Hamburg, and the Musikverein in Vienna. In 2019, I performed Tchaikovsky's Violin Concerto with the orchestra, conducted by Daisuke Soga. In 2023, I was selected for the Be Phil Orchestra 2023 Japan, organized by the Berliner Philharmoniker, and performed under the baton of Kirill Petrenko, alongside Daishin Kashimoto, Ludwig Quandt, and other distinguished musicians. I have studied with Fumi Koseki and Yasuko Ohtani and participated in masterclasses with members of the Bayreuth Festival Quartet, as well as Mayumi Kanagawa, Reiko Watanabe, Simon James, and Sulki Yu. I have also participated in the Chofu International Music Festival and various music festivals and masterclasses in Japan and abroad, including in the United States and Italy.
